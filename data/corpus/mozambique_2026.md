# Mozambique — Food Security Outlook (Synthetic, Oct 2025–May 2026 style)

Region: Cabo Delgado, Nampula (northern provinces)
IPC Phase: Crisis (Phase 3) in conflict-affected districts; seasonal improvement expected with harvest
Primary drivers: Non-state armed group activity causing renewed displacement, harvest-driven seasonal improvement in accessible areas, ongoing humanitarian food assistance in the most affected districts.

Seasonal improvement is expected from April-May as the main harvest is collected, increasing food consumption and income from crop sales in accessible zones. However, non-state armed group attacks intensified between July and September in northern districts, causing renewed displacement of thousands and disrupting typical subsistence farming and income-generating activities in Nangade, Muidumbe, Macomia, and Quissanga.

Outlook: Improvement is likely to remain partial and geographically uneven — accessible districts should see gains from the harvest, while conflict-affected districts will likely require continued humanitarian food assistance through at least April.
