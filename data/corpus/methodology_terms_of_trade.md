# Methodology Note — Pastoral Terms of Trade

The pastoral terms of trade (ToT) indicator measures the quantity of cereal a herder can purchase with the sale of one head of livestock (e.g. one goat), relative to the historical average. A declining ToT — meaning fewer kilograms of cereal obtainable per animal sold — signals deteriorating purchasing power for pastoralist households and is a leading indicator of food access stress in pastoral and agropastoral zones, often preceding formal IPC phase upgrades by several weeks to months.
