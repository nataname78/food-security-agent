# Ethiopia — Food Security Outlook (Synthetic, Feb–Sep 2026 style)

Region: Southern and southeastern lowlands (pastoral and agropastoral zones)
IPC Phase: Crisis (Phase 3) in lowland pastoral areas; Stressed (Phase 2) in most cropping highlands
Primary drivers: Elevated probability of El Niño development coinciding with the June–September kiremt rains, conflict in parts of the north limiting humanitarian access, lean-season food access decline.

Seasonal forecast centers estimate an elevated chance (roughly 50-60%) of a transition from ENSO-neutral to El Niño conditions during the kiremt season, which historically correlates with reduced rainfall in southern pastoral zones and above-average rainfall in some western cropping areas. Pastoralist households are the population of highest concern, given reduced pasture and water access following two below-average rainy seasons.

Northern conflict-affected zones continue to see constrained humanitarian access, limiting the ability to verify and respond to reported acute food insecurity.

Outlook: If El Niño materializes as projected, southern pastoral areas face an elevated risk of deterioration; western agricultural zones could see localized production gains offsetting national deficits.
