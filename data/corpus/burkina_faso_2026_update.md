# Burkina Faso — Food Security Outlook Update (Synthetic, Apr–Sep 2026 style)

Region: Sourou, Bankui, Yaaga, Kuilsé (northern and central-north provinces)
IPC Phase: Crisis (Phase 3) in conflict-affected communes; Stressed (Phase 2) in recovering central/southern zones
Primary drivers: New population displacement (over 30,000 newly displaced in Q1 2026), continued insecurity limiting field access, partial recovery in previously contested zones.

Displacement continues to be the dominant driver of acute food insecurity in the north. In parallel, the security situation has improved in southern and central provinces since 2024, with defense forces and local volunteer units supporting the return of displaced populations to some communes, partially restoring agricultural access.

Market functioning remains uneven: domestic cereal and fertilizer prices are exposed to global supply shocks (shipping disruptions, fertilizer cost increases), which could raise production costs for the 2026/27 season if input access does not improve before planting.

Outlook: Food consumption gaps are expected to persist in communes with active conflict through the outlook period, while recovering zones may see gradual improvement toward Minimal/Stressed outcomes if the security trend holds.
