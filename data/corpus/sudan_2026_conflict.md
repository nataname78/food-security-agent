# Sudan — Food Security Outlook (Synthetic, Jun 2026–Jan 2027 style)

Region: North Darfur, West Darfur, Greater Kordofan (including El-Obeid area), Blue Nile
IPC Phase: Emergency (Phase 4), Famine risk in worst-affected localities
Primary drivers: Active conflict frontlines, fragmented armed group control, disruption of trade and humanitarian corridors, drone-intensive warfare affecting areas previously considered safer.

Conflict dynamics have shifted since early 2026, with increased use of long-range strikes affecting areas that were previously distant from active frontlines. This has expanded the geographic footprint of acute food insecurity beyond the immediate conflict zones. Humanitarian access remains severely constrained in besieged and contested localities.

Market and supply chain disruption is compounding the conflict-driven access crisis: staple cereal availability in besieged towns is reported to be critically low, with reliance on informal and unofficial supply routes.

Outlook: Without a substantial improvement in humanitarian access or a reduction in hostilities, Emergency conditions are expected to persist, with a credible risk of Famine (Phase 5) in the most isolated, besieged localities.
